# Engineering Design & Calculations

Engineering calculations and design support for buildings, industrial facilities, equipment shelters and infrastructure projects.

## Structural Analysis & Design

**Category:** Structural Engineering  
**Software:** SAP2000 or STAAD.Pro

We provide structural analysis and design for steel and reinforced concrete structures, including buildings, shelters, equipment supports and platforms. Using SAP2000 or STAAD.Pro, we assess gravity, wind and seismic loads, check strength and deflection, and prepare calculation reports to support fabrication and construction.

## HVAC Load Calculations & Equipment Sizing

**Category:** Mechanical Engineering  
**Software:** Carrier HAP or Elite CHVAC

We calculate cooling and heating loads for commercial buildings, industrial facilities, electrical rooms and equipment shelters using Carrier HAP or Elite CHVAC. Our studies account for local climate, building materials, occupancy, ventilation and equipment heat gains to determine required capacity and airflow, with reports supporting equipment selection.

## Foundation & Anchorage Design

**Category:** Civil & Structural Engineering  
**Software:** SAFE; IDEA StatiCa for connection and local concrete checks, where applicable

We design reinforced concrete foundations for buildings, steel structures and equipment supports using SAFE, with IDEA StatiCa for base plate, anchorage and local concrete checks where applicable. Our calculations use structural reactions and geotechnical inputs to assess bearing pressure, strength and reinforcement requirements.

## Lighting Calculations & Design

**Category:** Electrical & Lighting Engineering  
**Software:** DIALux evo or AGi32

We provide indoor and outdoor lighting design for commercial buildings, industrial facilities, warehouses, streets and parking areas. Using DIALux evo or AGi32, we calculate illuminance (lux), uniformity and glare, optimize luminaire layouts, and prepare photometric reports, lighting schedules and 3D visualizations to support equipment selection and project review.
